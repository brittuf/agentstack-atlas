Smith.ai for dental & medical clinics: setup outline and ROI preset

Smith.ai's free tier lets a dental office test an AI receptionist on real patient calls without paying, and its managed Enterprise tier suits practices with nobody to tune prompts. Patient-data rules make the setup order different from other trades.

What Smith.ai documents that matters here

  • Smith.ai's AI receptionist pricing page lists a Free plan with 25 calls a month ($3.00 per call after that), Pro at $150 for 75 calls, $270 for 150 and $500 for 300, and Enterprise at $500 for 300 calls or $800 for 500, with custom pricing above. Source official page fetched, 2026-10-28
  • Extra calls cost $2.50, $2.30 or $2.17 on the Pro tiers and $2.10 on Enterprise 500; Enterprise is built and run by Smith.ai, with custom prompting, integrations and multi-location support. Source official page fetched, 2026-10-28
  • Every plan lists AI scheduling (Calendly and more), call recording, transcription and summaries, and a Quality Studio for testing before going live. Source official page fetched, 2026-10-28

Why it fits dental & medical clinics

Dental front desks juggle new-patient calls, reschedules, insurance questions and the occasional emergency toothache, often while checking a patient in. Per-call pricing suits that mix, since a long insurance conversation costs the same as a quick confirmation. The Quality Studio matters more here than in most trades: test calls let you hear how the agent handles someone asking about pain or medication before a real patient does. (Our analysis.)

What it costs at dental & medical clinics volumes

Our arithmetic from the posted rates: a practice with about 400 calls a month would pay $500 for Pro 300 plus 100 extra calls at $2.17, about $717, against $800 for Enterprise 500. At 800 calls, Enterprise 500 plus 300 calls at $2.10 comes to about $1,430.

Setup outline for dental & medical clinics

  1. Before any patient call flows through it, ask Smith.ai in writing whether it will sign a HIPAA business associate agreement for the AI receptionist and on which plan. Do not assume.
  2. Start on the free plan with Quality Studio test calls: new patient, reschedule, insurance question, toothache, billing.
  3. Write the rules for clinical questions: the agent never gives medical advice and routes pain, swelling or bleeding to the on-call dentist or a priority callback list.
  4. Check where bookings land: directly in your practice management system, in a scheduling tool, or as a message for the front desk.
  5. Go live on after-hours and lunch-hour calls first, when the desk is unstaffed.
  6. After the free 25 calls, read every transcript and estimate monthly volume before choosing a Pro tier or the managed Enterprise setup.

Watch-outs

  • Settle HIPAA questions in writing first; this is not legal advice.
  • Practice management sync is the make-or-break integration; confirm it before paying.
  • Patients returning reminder calls add to the per-call count.
  • Keep a human path for distressed callers.
