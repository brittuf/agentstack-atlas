Retell AI for dental & medical clinics: setup outline and ROI preset

Most self-serve AI receptionists we track list a HIPAA BAA only on enterprise plans or not at all. Retell's docs say its BAA can be self-signed at no extra fee, which makes it the developer platform to look at when a clinic, or the agency building for one, needs a phone agent that may hear patient details.

What Retell AI documents that matters here

  • Retell's compliance docs say it is HIPAA compliant, that a signed BAA is required before transmitting PHI, and that the BAA and DPA are available for self-signing at no additional fee. Source official page fetched, 2026-11-18
  • The same docs describe per-agent data retention from 1 day to 2 years for transcripts, recordings and logs, and per-agent storage settings to exclude PII or keep only basic attributes. Source official page fetched, 2026-11-18
  • Retell's Cal.com tools can check availability, book, list, reschedule and cancel appointments live during a call, with no synced copy of the calendar kept in Retell. Source official page fetched, 2026-11-18
  • Its pricing page lists PII removal at +$0.01/min and US Twilio telephony at $0.015/min, on top of $0.055/min voice infrastructure. Source official page fetched, 2026-11-18

Why it fits dental & medical clinics

Dental and medical offices spend a large share of front-desk time on scheduling: new patients, cleanings, reschedules and cancellations. Retell's booking tools cover that full cycle in one call, and the transfer tool's warm mode lets the agent brief a staff member before handing over a caller with a clinical question. The compliance controls are the reason to build rather than buy here: a signed BAA, short retention and PII exclusion are settings you control per agent. (Our analysis.)

What it costs at dental & medical clinics volumes

Our arithmetic from Retell's component prices: Retell infrastructure ($0.055), a platform voice ($0.015), GPT 6.1 Terra ($0.032), Twilio US ($0.015), knowledge base retrieval (+$0.005) and PII removal (+$0.01) total $0.132 a minute. At 1,200 minutes a month that is $158.40, plus $2 for a Retell number, about $160. A builder's setup fee is extra and not included.

Setup outline for dental & medical clinics

  1. Sign the BAA in Retell's click-through agreements before any test call uses real patient information.
  2. Decide the scope: scheduling and general questions only, with anything clinical transferred to staff.
  3. Connect the clinic's Cal.com event types, or build a custom function if the practice-management system has an API.
  4. Set retention to the shortest period the practice allows and exclude PII from storage where possible.
  5. Run simulation tests covering new patients, reschedules, insurance questions and an emergency caller.
  6. Go live on after-hours calls first, with a human review of every booked appointment for two weeks.

Our suggested order, not vendor documentation.

Watch-outs

  • The BAA covers Retell; check that your own telephony, calendar and any webhook targets are also covered.
  • Cal.com tools are pinned to one event type each; a clinic with many appointment types needs several tools.
  • Prompts over 4,000 tokens bill extra time, so keep the clinic's knowledge in the knowledge base, not the prompt.
  • Someone has to own the agent after launch; budget for changes when hours or insurers change.
