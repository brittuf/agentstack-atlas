Tidio Lyro for auto repair shops: setup outline and ROI preset

Auto shops field the same written questions all day: do you work on hybrids, how soon can you look at a check-engine light, is my car ready. Tidio's Lyro can take the general ones from the shop's website and hand status or estimate questions to the service desk, and Tidio's own pricing page features a car body repair customer as a case study.

Why it fits auto repair shops

Service advisors lose most of their day to interruptions, and the website chat is a second front desk nobody staffs. Many messages are answerable from a page the shop already has: which makes it services, whether it does state inspections, loaner policy, warranty terms, the diagnostic fee. The questions that need a person, such as where a specific repair stands, are better handed off than guessed, unless the shop management system exposes an API or MCP server Lyro can query. That makes Lyro a filter in front of the advisors rather than a replacement for them, and a conversation quota is easy for an owner to budget. (Our analysis.)

What it costs at auto repair shops volumes

Our arithmetic from Tidio's posted prices: a shop answering about 100 website chats, Messenger threads and emails a month fits AI-enabled Growth at $75 on monthly billing, or $62.50 a month on annual billing. Doubling the volume moves the quota to 200 conversations at $140. Seats are not the meter, and Starter and Growth allow up to 10 people.

Setup outline for auto repair shops

  1. List the services you do and do not offer, by make and repair type, on one website page Lyro can read.
  2. Publish the diagnostic fee, warranty terms, loaner and shuttle policy, and hours for drop-off and pickup.
  3. Connect the website chat and Facebook Messenger, and route repair-status questions to the advisor queue.
  4. If your shop software has an API or MCP server, test one Smart Action that looks up a repair order by phone number.
  5. Spend the 50 free Lyro conversations during a normal week and read every handoff.
  6. Choose Starter or Growth from the real conversation count, not the AI share.

Watch-outs

  • The 89% figure is Tidio's case study, not a benchmark; expect lower until your website answers the common questions.
  • Never let the agent quote a repair price it has not been given in writing.
  • Repair-status lookups need an integration; without one, make sure Lyro hands off instead of guessing.
  • Advisor replies in chat use the same quota as Lyro.
