AI agents for auto repair shops: what's real in 2026

Shops lose calls while advisors are busy at the counter or in the bay. AI agents mostly cover answering, vehicle intake and booking.

What vendors document today

  • Lippy (vendor) says its agent captures the vehicle and the concern, books against real availability, confirms by text, and texts back missed calls. Lippy automotive page source via search extract
  • AgentZap (vendor) says it captures year, make, model, mileage and symptoms and syncs with Tekmetric, Mitchell 1 and Shop-Ware. AgentZap auto repair page source via search extract
  • Lineshift notes that drop-off time, work time and pickup time are different things, and that an agent should follow the shop's configured booking rules (e.g. Tekmetric settings). Lineshift article source via search extract

Checklist before you buy or build

  • Does it book into the scheduling system you already use, or just take messages?
  • What triggers a hand-off to a human (emergencies, angry callers, pricing questions)?
  • Can you review call transcripts and outcomes?
  • Start with after-hours or overflow calls, then expand.
  • Which services can be quoted on the phone, and which need a tech to look first?

Our view (opinion): Start with booking routine services (oil changes, inspections) and send anything diagnostic to a person.
