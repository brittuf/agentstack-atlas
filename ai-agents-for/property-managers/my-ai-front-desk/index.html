My AI Front Desk for property managers: setup outline and ROI preset

My AI Front Desk bundles calls, web chat, texting and a built-in CRM in one plan. For a property manager juggling tenant calls, leasing inquiries and overdue-rent follow-ups, the interest is the follow-up automation as much as the answering.

What My AI Front Desk documents that matters here

  • The pricing page lists Business-in-a-Box at $99/month ($79 billed annually) with 200 voice minutes, 100 chatbot conversations and 400 SMS a month, plus 1,000 monthly overage credits; extra voice minutes cost 25 credits ($0.25). Source official page fetched, 2026-11-11
  • It caps outbound AI calls at 20 a day on that plan, and lists an AI-native CRM, sequence automations and Zapier among the included features; Partner/Enterprise is custom. Source official page fetched, 2026-11-11
  • A customer story on the page, from Zest Properties' COO, says a delinquency call list that normally took all day was done in five minutes (a vendor-published testimonial). Source official page fetched, 2026-11-11

Why it fits property managers

Property managers rarely only answer phones. Rent reminders, delinquency follow-ups and showing confirmations go out by text and call, so a plan that includes SMS and sequence automations covers more of the job than an answering service alone. The 20-per-day outbound cap is a limit to respect: a portfolio with a long delinquency list may exceed it. The CRM can also double as a record of tenant contacts, if you are willing to keep it alongside your property-management software. (Our analysis.)

What it costs at property managers volumes

Our arithmetic: 300 calls at 3 minutes is 900 minutes. 200 are included; the other 700 minutes at $0.25 is $175, of which the monthly 1,000-credit pool covers about $10, so roughly $99 plus $165, near $264 a month. Texts and chats also draw on credits if you exceed their allowances.

Setup outline for property managers

  1. Separate inbound tenant calls from outbound follow-up work, since the plan limits and costs differ.
  2. Start the 7-day trial and load the knowledge base (20 pages on this plan) with lease basics, office hours and maintenance process.
  3. Set up inbound calls first, with escalation of emergencies to your on-call phone.
  4. Then add one outbound sequence, such as a rent reminder text, within the 20-call daily cap.
  5. Decide whether the CRM will mirror your property-management system or only hold new leads.
  6. Check credit use after two weeks and compare against a larger plan.

Watch-outs

  • Automated delinquency calls and texts are regulated; confirm rules with counsel before enabling them.
  • A second CRM next to your PM software creates double entry unless you decide what lives where.
  • The 20-a-day outbound cap is a hard limit on this plan.
  • Overage credits can come from voice, SMS and chat together; watch the pool.
