Freshdesk Freddy AI Agent for property managers: setup outline and ROI preset

Property managers live in email: maintenance requests, move-out questions, rent portal problems, pet policy, parking. Freshdesk's Email AI Agent reads each incoming ticket, answers from the policies you load, and passes the rest to staff, and a whole email thread within 72 hours counts as one billable session.

What Freshdesk Freddy AI Agent documents that matters here

  • Freshdesk's setup guide says the Email AI Agent works in the ticket inbox, answers from your knowledge base, and either resolves the ticket or escalates it to a human agent. Source official page fetched, 2026-11-25
  • The pricing FAQ says an email AI session is a 72-hour window from the customer's first email, so every AI reply in that window is one session; extra sessions are $49 per 100 after a one-time 500. Source official page fetched, 2026-11-25
  • The knowledge guide allows up to 200 uploaded files of up to 35 MB each per AI agent, plus published solution articles, custom Q&A pairs and Google Drive or SharePoint sources. Source official page fetched, 2026-11-25

Why it fits property managers

Tenant email is a mix of routine questions with written answers and maintenance requests that need a work order. The routine half covers how to pay rent, when the pool opens, the move-out checklist, guest parking rules and the pet deposit, and it all sits in lease addenda and community handbooks a manager can upload as files. An email agent that answers those from the documents, and opens or escalates a ticket for everything else, shortens the queue without touching the parts that carry liability. The 72-hour session rule matters here because tenant threads often go three or four rounds before they are settled. (Our analysis.)

What it costs at property managers volumes

Our arithmetic from Freshdesk's posted prices: two Growth seats at $19 a month each (annual) plus about 300 AI email sessions a month. After the one-time 500 free sessions run out, 300 sessions cost three packs of 100 at $49, $147, so the monthly bill is about $185. A portfolio with 600 sessions a month would pay $294 in packs on the same two seats.

Setup outline for property managers

  1. Collect the community handbook, lease addenda, move-in and move-out checklists and rent payment instructions as PDF or Word files.
  2. Publish the tenant FAQ as solution articles visible to all users, since private articles are excluded.
  3. Create one Email AI Agent for the resident mailbox and add the files and articles as knowledge.
  4. Write instructions that any leak, no heat, lockout, gas smell or safety issue goes straight to the on-call staff, not an AI reply.
  5. Test with last month's real tenant emails in the studio before deploying.
  6. Track how fast the free 500 sessions go, then buy packs to match your real rate.

Watch-outs

  • Emergency maintenance must never wait on an AI reply; route it by keyword before the agent sees it.
  • Sessions are billed whether or not the agent resolves them, so weak handbooks cost money.
  • Fair-housing rules apply to every answer about applicants; keep screening questions with staff.
  • The 500 free sessions are a one-time allowance, not monthly.
