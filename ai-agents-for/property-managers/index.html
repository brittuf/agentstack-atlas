AI agents for property managers: what's real in 2026

Property management is one of the busiest areas for agentic AI: leasing inquiries, tour scheduling, maintenance requests and collections.

What vendors document today

  • AppFolio says its Realm-X Leasing Performer answers email, text and now phone calls 24/7, schedules tours and works natively inside AppFolio. It also announced a Realm-X connector with Anthropic's Claude (June 16, 2026). AppFolio newsroom source via search extract
  • EliseAI publishes which products (Leasing, Voice, Delinquency, Maintenance, Renewals) work with which PMS/CRM (e.g. Yardi Voyager, Entrata, AppFolio Plus/Max). EliseAI integrations source via search extract

Checklist before you buy or build

  • Does it book into the scheduling system you already use, or just take messages?
  • What triggers a hand-off to a human (emergencies, angry callers, pricing questions)?
  • Can you review call transcripts and outcomes?
  • Start with after-hours or overflow calls, then expand.
  • Check your PMS/CRM against the vendor's integration table.
  • Make sure fair-housing-sensitive questions are handled safely.

Our view (opinion): Small portfolios can often start with an AI text/phone assistant for showing requests before buying an enterprise platform.
