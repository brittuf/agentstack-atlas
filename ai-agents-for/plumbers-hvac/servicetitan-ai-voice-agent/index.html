ServiceTitan AI Voice Agent for plumbers & HVAC: setup outline and ROI preset

If your shop already runs ServiceTitan on an eligible package, its built-in voice agent is the first AI receptionist to test, because it books into the same board your CSRs use. This page turns ServiceTitan's documentation into a setup order for a plumbing or HVAC shop, plus a calculator preset you can adjust.

What ServiceTitan AI Voice Agent documents that matters here

  • ServiceTitan's FAQ says the agent books new jobs, including after hours, using Adaptive Capacity, confirms and reschedules appointments, and recognises members so eligible jobs are booked against recurring service events. Source official page fetched, 2026-10-28
  • It can text a secure payment link when a caller wants to pay an outstanding invoice (ST Payments must be enabled), and escalates to a live agent when the caller asks for one or the system detects frustration. Source official page fetched, 2026-10-28
  • The FAQ says it is designed for US and Canada residential home-service companies on The Essentials and The Works packages with an active Basic Phone integration or Phones Pro, and that accounts can run several independent agents, each with its own persona, job types and escalation settings. Source official page fetched, 2026-10-28

Why it fits plumbers & HVAC

Plumbing and HVAC calls sort into a few types that drive revenue: emergency no-heat or burst-pipe calls, routine repairs, membership tune-ups and billing. ServiceTitan's agent covers most of them natively. Membership recognition is the quiet win: a member calling about a spring AC tune-up is booked against their recurring service event, so the visit counts toward the plan instead of becoming a one-off job someone must fix later. Separate agents let a shop give night calls their own greeting and stricter escalation. (Our analysis.)

What it costs at plumbers & HVAC volumes

ServiceTitan does not publish what the voice agent costs; it depends on your package. That is why the preset below leaves the agent cost at the calculator's $300 placeholder: replace it with your quote.

Setup outline for plumbers & HVAC

  1. Confirm with your ServiceTitan rep that your package and phone setup (Basic Phone integration or Phones Pro) qualify, and get the price in writing.
  2. Audit your job types before switching anything on. The agent infers job types from what callers say, so merge duplicates and write plain descriptions for no-cool, no-heat, leak, drain and water-heater calls.
  3. Set escalation triggers for gas smells, flooding, no heat below a temperature you choose, angry callers and price disputes, routed to the on-call tech.
  4. Create a separate after-hours agent so night calls get a different greeting and stricter escalation than daytime overflow.
  5. Enable invoice payment links only if ST Payments is live, and tell the office that some billing calls will now resolve without them.
  6. Run after-hours only for two weeks, listen to every booked call and check each job type against what a CSR would have chosen before adding daytime overflow.

Watch-outs

  • Commercial work: the documentation describes residential home-service use, so keep a human path for commercial accounts.
  • Job-type errors are the costly failure; a mis-booked call sends the wrong tech or the wrong truck stock.
  • Decide what the agent says about dispatch fees and after-hours rates before go-live, since it communicates dispatch fees.
  • Callers who speak Spanish get Spanish automatically, so make sure someone can take Spanish escalations.
