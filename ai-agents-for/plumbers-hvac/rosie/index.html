Rosie for plumbers & HVAC: setup outline and ROI preset

For a plumber or HVAC owner who is also the on-call tech, the interesting Rosie feature is not the price but the waterfall: it can try several numbers in order until a human answers. That feature sits on the highest tier.

What Rosie documents that matters here

  • Rosie lists Professional at $49/month (250 minutes), Scale at $149 (1,000 minutes) and Growth at $299 (2,000 minutes); a 7-day free trial is offered. Source official page fetched, 2026-11-11
  • Scale adds warm transfers, where Rosie briefs your team before connecting, and live call transfers; Growth adds waterfall transfers that try multiple numbers until someone answers. Source official page fetched, 2026-11-11
  • All plans include handling multiple calls at once, English and Spanish on every call, call summaries and recordings, email and text notifications for every call and a Zapier integration. Source official page fetched, 2026-11-11

Why it fits plumbers & HVAC

A small mechanical shop has a handful of people who might pick up at night: the owner, a lead tech and maybe a spouse. A waterfall tries each in turn, which is more reliable than one forwarded number. Scale's warm transfer means the tech hears the issue before answering. Notifications by text and email on every call also make a good paper trail for dispatch. Spanish on every plan will matter for crews serving mixed neighborhoods. (Our analysis.)

What it costs at plumbers & HVAC volumes

Our arithmetic: 120 after-hours calls at 4 minutes is 480 minutes a month, within Scale's 1,000 at $149. Growth at $299 is the first plan with waterfall transfers; it is a decision about reliability, not minutes. The page text we checked did not state an overage rate.

Setup outline for plumbers & HVAC

  1. List everyone who could answer an emergency and in what order; that is your waterfall if you choose Growth.
  2. Start the free trial on Scale, run after-hours only and use warm transfer to the on-call phone.
  3. Write the question list: address, callback number, what is leaking or not working, whether it is an emergency.
  4. Test with five scripted calls including Spanish, a gas smell and a quote request.
  5. Use Zapier to send summaries to your dispatch tool or a shared sheet.
  6. If calls ring out during the trial, price Growth for the waterfall.

Our suggested order, not vendor documentation.

Watch-outs

  • Waterfall transfers are Growth only; the page describes Scale transfers as warm and live, not as a multi-number chase.
  • No overage rate was visible in the text we checked; ask before busy season.
  • Text-based summaries need someone to read them first thing in the morning.
  • Keep the knowledge base current on service area and emergency rates.
