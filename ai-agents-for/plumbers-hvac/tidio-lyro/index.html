Tidio Lyro for plumbers & HVAC: setup outline and ROI preset

For a plumbing or HVAC company, the website chat bubble is where a homeowner decides at 10 p.m. whether to call you or the next listing. Tidio pairs scripted Flows that collect a name, address and problem with the Lyro AI agent for open questions, and its Starter plan is priced for a shop that gets a few dozen web conversations a month.

Why it fits plumbers & HVAC

Web leads for home services are perishable. A homeowner with a leaking water heater or a furnace that will not light fills in whichever form answers first, and most small shops reply the next morning. A Flow can ask the qualifying questions every dispatcher asks, such as zip code, type of system, how urgent, and best callback number, and Lyro can answer what the website already says about service area, trip fees, financing and maintenance plans. Emergencies still need a phone call, so the agent's job is to collect a clean lead and point urgent cases at the on-call number rather than promise a time. (Our analysis.)

What it costs at plumbers & HVAC volumes

Our arithmetic from Tidio's posted prices: a shop with about 45 web chats and Messenger threads a month fits AI-enabled Starter at $39 on monthly billing, or $32.50 a month billed annually. The 100 free Flows visitors cover a small site; past that the Flows add-on starts at $24 a month. If web conversations pass 50, Growth starts at $75 for 100.

Setup outline for plumbers & HVAC

  1. Write one page that states your service area by town or zip, trip fee, hours and emergency policy.
  2. Build a Flow that asks for zip code, equipment type, urgency and a callback number before anything else.
  3. Point Lyro at the website and add Guidance that any gas smell, active leak or no-heat call gets the on-call number immediately.
  4. Send every captured lead to the office inbox or your field-service tool by email or Zapier.
  5. Run it for two weeks on the free Lyro allowance and count how many leads arrived after hours.
  6. Pick Starter or Growth from the real conversation count, and add the Flows add-on only if visitors pass 100.

Watch-outs

  • Chat is not an emergency line; make the on-call phone number the first answer to any urgent keyword.
  • Flows visitors and conversations are separate limits, so a busy Flow can cost extra even if chats stay low.
  • Lyro answers only from what you publish, so outdated trip fees on the website become outdated answers.
  • Leads collected in chat still need a person to call back quickly; set an alert.
