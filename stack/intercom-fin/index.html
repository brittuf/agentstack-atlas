Intercom Fin in real AI agent deployments

Intercom's Fin AI Agent answers customers over chat, email, phone and social messaging, and Intercom bills it per outcome instead of per seat or per message. No catalog deployment names Fin; it does the job our Commerzbank entry describes (a chatbot resolving most of two million chats) at the scale of a small or mid-size support team.

  • Commerzbank "Bene": a chatbot that handles 2M+ chats and resolves 70% of them.
  • NoBroker ConvoZen (in the catalog, source): multilingual support automation (10k hours of recordings per day).

What the source says

  • Intercom's pricing page lists three plans that all include Fin: Essential at $29 per seat a month on annual billing (shown at $19 for new customers), Advanced at $85 and Expert at $132, each plus $0.99 per Fin outcome; Fin Voice outcomes are $1.99. Source official page fetched, 2026-11-25
  • The pricing FAQ defines an outcome as a customer confirming the issue is resolved, a customer not asking for more help after Fin responds, or Fin completing a workflow (a Procedure) including handoffs, and says each conversation is charged only once. Source official page fetched, 2026-11-25
  • For teams that keep another helpdesk, the page sells Fin alone at $0.99 per outcome with no seat costs and no setup or platform fees, subject to a minimum monthly commitment it illustrates as 50 outcomes; Salesforce is the named example. Source official page fetched, 2026-11-25
  • Intercom's Fin FAQ lists the Messenger on web, iOS and Android, email, WhatsApp, SMS, Facebook and Instagram as supported channels, says Fin can be limited to set times such as office hours, and says public website content re-syncs weekly. Source official page fetched, 2026-11-25
  • The pricing FAQ lists a 14-day free trial with no credit card, a minimum of one Full seat, and places HIPAA support on the Expert plan. Source official page fetched, 2026-11-25

Where it fits in an agent build

Fin makes the most sense where written help content already exists, because it answers from your articles, PDFs and synced pages and can scope answers by audience. By our arithmetic, two Essential seats plus 400 Fin outcomes come to $58 plus $396, about $454 a month, so past a few hundred resolutions the outcome meter, not the seats, decides the bill. The seatless option is the unusual part: a team that already lives in Salesforce or another helpdesk can trial the AI layer without moving its agents, as long as it clears the monthly minimum. (Our analysis.)

When to look elsewhere

Per-outcome billing rewards a strict definition of success, and Intercom's includes conversations where the customer simply stops asking. If your customers tend to give up rather than reply, expect to pay for some unhappy endings. A shop with little written help content will see little from Fin until that content exists. And clinics that need HIPAA support must buy Expert seats at $132 each, which is steep for a front desk of two or three. (Our analysis.)

Questions to ask before you buy

  • How many of last month's billed outcomes later reopened or came back on another channel?
  • What is the exact monthly minimum for the seatless Fin-on-your-helpdesk option?
  • Will Intercom sign a BAA on Expert, and what does HIPAA support cover?
  • Can we cap Fin outcomes per month so the bill can't run past a set amount?

A setup outline

  1. Export three months of conversations and tag the top 20 questions by volume; those are what Fin must answer well.
  2. Write or tidy one help article per top question before turning Fin on; Fin answers from content, not guesswork.
  3. Start the 14-day trial without a card and test Fin as a teammate preview before customers see it.
  4. Limit Fin to after-hours or one channel first, using the schedule controls in the FAQ.
  5. Set a monthly outcome budget and review every billed outcome for the first two weeks.
  6. Decide on Essential, Advanced or the seatless helpdesk option only after you know your monthly outcome count.
