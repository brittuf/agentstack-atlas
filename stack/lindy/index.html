Where it fits in an agent build

Lindy fits the person-level version of the internal-ops pattern: one assistant that triages email, prepares meeting notes and answers routine questions in Slack, rather than a company-wide help desk. ShipStation's Deckhand and Workday's HR skills show what that looks like at scale; a small office manager or founder can get a slice of it without building anything. Neither source names Lindy. (Our analysis.)

When to look elsewhere

Credits that do not roll over make light, irregular use expensive per task. If what you need is a single repeatable workflow, a Zapier or Make automation is easier to predict. For patient data, confirm the Enterprise BAA before connecting anything clinical. (Our analysis.)

A setup outline

  1. Pick one inbox or calendar job that eats an hour a day.
  2. Start on the free credits and write down every task you hand over for a week.
  3. Compare credits used with the Team plan's 3,000 per seat to see if a paid seat makes sense.
  4. Keep sending permissions off until drafts are consistently right.
  5. Review the activity log weekly for anything the assistant did that you would not have done.
