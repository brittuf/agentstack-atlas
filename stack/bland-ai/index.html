What the source says

  • Bland's pricing page lists Start at $0.14/min with no platform fee (10 concurrent calls, 100 calls a day, 1 voice, no card required) and Build at $0.12/min plus a $299/month platform fee (50 concurrent calls, 2,000 calls a day, 5 voices); Enterprise is contracted to volume. Where it fits in an agent build

Bland's single rate is the easiest of the developer platforms to budget: there are no token or model pass-through lines, so finance can multiply minutes by one number plus telephony. That makes it a fit for outbound or high-volume campaigns where the script is fixed and the main variable is minutes. The Enterprise track, with on-prem or VPC deployment and a 28-day rollout, is aimed at regulated organizations that want a vendor team on the build. (Our analysis.)

When to look elsewhere

For a small business receptionist, the self-serve plans leave out the pieces that matter most: warm and live transfers, the appointment scheduling node, in-call SMS and the BAA are all Enterprise-only on the comparison table. The Start plan's 100-calls-a-day cap also limits a busy line. A receptionist from our tracker, or Retell or Vapi with their transfer and booking tools, will usually fit better below enterprise scale. (Our analysis.)

Questions to ask before you buy

  • What does Bland's built-in Twilio telephony cost per minute at pass-through?
  • Which Enterprise-only features would we need on day one?
  • Are the daily and hourly call caps on Start and Build hard limits?
  • What volume does Enterprise pricing start at?

A setup outline

  1. Decide whether you are buying a campaign tool (fixed script, many calls) or a receptionist (open questions, transfers); Bland's self-serve plans suit the first.
  2. Sign up on Start, which needs no card, and build one pathway with the 2 included credits.
  3. Bring your own Twilio or SIP trunk if you already have one, to avoid transfer fees.
  4. Measure average talk minutes and transfer minutes per call during a pilot.
  5. Compare a month at $0.14/min against $299 plus $0.12/min before moving to Build.
  6. Ask sales for Enterprise terms only if you need warm transfers, booking, BAA or SSO.
