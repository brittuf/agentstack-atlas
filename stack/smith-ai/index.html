Where it fits in an agent build

Smith.ai's free 25 calls a month is the lowest-risk way to see an AI receptionist handle your real callers. The other distinctive point is the managed Enterprise tier, where Smith.ai builds and runs the agent for you, which suits an owner with no time to tune prompts. Per-call pricing sits between per-minute and per-caller models: long calls do not cost more, but repeat callers do. (Our analysis.)

When to look elsewhere

Per-call prices are higher than the entry per-minute plans, so a business with hundreds of short calls may pay more here. If you want a partner or reseller arrangement, note that Smith.ai's partner programme is a referral and reseller model, not a flat affiliate rate. (Our analysis.)

A setup outline

  1. Start on the free plan with after-hours calls.
  2. Review every transcript from the first 25 calls.
  3. Note which calls the AI should have transferred, and adjust rules.
  4. Estimate monthly answered calls before picking a Pro tier.
  5. Decide whether you will tune it yourself or want the managed Enterprise setup.
