Where it fits in an agent build

EliseAI's integration table is the reason to look at it: it lets you check, product by product, whether it will read and write to the system you already run. That matters more than any feature list, because an agent that answers a maintenance request but cannot open the work order just moves the typing elsewhere. Operators on Yardi or Entrata, where native options differ from AppFolio's, are the clearest fit. The delinquency and renewal products extend agents into collections and retention, which are quieter but valuable jobs. (Our analysis.)

When to look elsewhere

If your PMS or your plan tier is not in the integration table, the value drops sharply. A small portfolio with low inquiry volume will likely not justify an enterprise suite. As with any resident-facing agent, collections and screening conversations carry legal rules; get them reviewed. (Our analysis.)

A setup outline

  1. Find your exact PMS and plan in EliseAI's integration table for each product you want.
  2. Pick one product (usually leasing) for a single-property pilot.
  3. Define which conversations must go to staff: legal notices, disputes, accommodation requests.
  4. Track response time, tours and work orders created against the prior period.
  5. Expand to delinquency or renewals only after the first product is stable.
