Where it fits in an agent build

Writesonic's current pitch is a loop: track which prompts in ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews mention you or a competitor, then generate articles and fixes aimed at those gaps. For a small brand the useful part is prompt tracking, because it replaces checking AI answers by hand every week. The agent workflows below Enterprise are trial runs, so treat them as a preview rather than the reason to buy. (Our analysis.)

When to look elsewhere

Perplexity, Claude and Copilot tracking are Enterprise-only, so a business whose buyers research on Perplexity will not see that on self-serve plans. If you only need articles, a writing tool costs less than visibility tracking. The headline prices are annual; check the monthly rate before committing. (Our analysis.)

Questions to ask before you buy

  • Which AI platforms are tracked on my tier, and how often are answers sampled?
  • How many agent workflow runs do I get, and what do full runs cost?
  • Can I export prompt-level history if I cancel?
  • Does the 7-day refund apply to annual plans?

A setup outline

  1. Write 30 to 50 real questions customers ask an AI assistant about your category; these become tracked prompts.
  2. Run the trial and record which prompts mention you, a competitor or nobody.
  3. Pick five prompts where a competitor is cited and you are not, and read the pages being cited.
  4. Publish or improve one page per prompt with real statistics and clear sections, then re-check weekly.
  5. Judge the subscription on movement in those five prompts after 60 days, not on article count.
