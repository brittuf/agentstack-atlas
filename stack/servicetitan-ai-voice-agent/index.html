Where it fits in an agent build

The advantage of a native agent is that it books into the real schedule with the real job types, memberships and dispatch fees, which general AI receptionists can only approximate through integrations. For a ServiceTitan shop, that removes the double entry that usually makes AI answering fail: the agent's booking is the booking. The job-type library is the important part; an agent that books a 'no cool' call as a maintenance visit sends the wrong tech. (Our analysis.)

When to look elsewhere

If you are not on ServiceTitan, or not on an eligible package, this is not available, and a general receptionist connected to your calendar will be quicker. Commercial-service companies should check eligibility, since the documentation describes residential use. (Our analysis.)

A setup outline

  1. Confirm your ServiceTitan package includes the voice agent.
  2. Review the job-type descriptions and edit them to match how your team actually books.
  3. Set escalation triggers for emergencies, angry callers and pricing disputes.
  4. Start with after-hours calls and listen to every recording in week one.
  5. Compare booked-job accuracy against your CSRs before expanding to daytime.
