Where it fits in an agent build

Tidio is the most budget-friendly shape for a small business that gets questions in the channels customers actually use: the website chat bubble, Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp. There is no per-resolution meter to model; you pick a conversation quota and every answered thread, by Lyro or by staff, uses one. By our arithmetic, AI-enabled Starter works out to $0.78 a conversation and Growth at 1,000 conversations to $0.70, which is below most per-resolution prices in our tracker. Flows handle scripted jobs such as collecting a phone number, and Lyro handles open questions. (Our analysis.)

When to look elsewhere

Because human replies use the same quota, a busy team can exhaust it even if Lyro is barely used. Teams above 10 seats or 1,000 conversations a month are pushed to Plus, which starts at $300 plus usage. Health practices should note that SOC 2 and HIPAA appear only under Tidio's custom tailored-solution offer, not on Starter or Growth. (Our analysis.)

Questions to ask before you buy

  • What happens to conversations that arrive after we hit the quota with automatic upgrades off?
  • Do Lyro Smart Actions and MCP tools count against the conversation quota when they run?
  • Is a BAA available below the custom tailored-solution tier?
  • How does the Voice add-on hand a call to a person when Lyro cannot help?

A setup outline

  1. Count last month's answered chats, DMs and support emails; that total, not the AI share, sets your quota.
  2. Install the chat widget and connect Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp and the support inbox.
  3. Point Lyro at your website, FAQ and any policy pages, then add a few Guidance instructions for tone and limits.
  4. Spend the 50 free Lyro conversations on real traffic before choosing Starter or Growth.
  5. Build one Flow for lead capture or routing if visitors often leave without asking anything.
  6. Turn on automatic upgrades only if you would rather pay more than stop answering.
