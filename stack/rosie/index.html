Where it fits in an agent build

Rosie's pricing has a split worth noticing: the entry plan texts the caller a booking link, while Scale and above book the appointment during the call. For a salon or clinic whose customers happily book online, the link is enough. For a plumber whose customers want a time slot confirmed before they hang up, it is not. Spanish on every plan is useful for businesses in bilingual areas without paying extra. (Our analysis.)

When to look elsewhere

Because no overage rate was shown on the pricing page, ask what happens when you exceed your minutes before a busy season. Businesses with long, technical calls should compare per-call or per-caller pricing. (Our analysis.)

A setup outline

  1. Decide whether a texted booking link is good enough for your customers, since that decides the plan.
  2. Count monthly talk minutes from your phone records.
  3. Trial it with a few calls in both English and Spanish if you serve both.
  4. Ask Rosie in writing how minutes beyond your plan are billed.
  5. Check how bookings land in your calendar before switching daytime calls over.
