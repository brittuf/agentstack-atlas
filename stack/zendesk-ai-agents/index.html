Agent stack › AI agents bundled into Zendesk Suite plans, billed per verified automated resolution

Zendesk AI agents in real AI agent deployments

Zendesk includes AI agents in its Suite and Support plans and charges for them per automated resolution, with a small allowance per human agent seat. No catalog deployment names Zendesk; ShipStation's after-hours help-desk agent and NoBroker's support automation are the nearest patterns.

Where Zendesk AI agents appears in our catalog

None of the 52 sourced deployments we track names Zendesk AI agents. We say so rather than imply otherwise.

Deployments with the same pattern

Our analysis: these deployments do the kind of job Zendesk AI agents is built for. Their sources do not say they used it.

  • ShipStation "Deckhand": a Slack IT help-desk agent that opens Jira tickets and answers 94% of after-hours questions.
  • NoBroker ConvoZen (in the catalog, source): multilingual support automation (10k hours of recordings per day).

What the source says

  • Zendesk's pricing page lists Support Team at $19 per agent a month on annual billing ($25 monthly), Suite Team at $55 ($69) and Suite Professional at $115 ($149); Suite Team is the first tier that lists AI Agents, messaging and live chat, and telephony. Source official page fetched, 2026-11-25
  • Each plan includes automated resolutions for AI agents: 5 per agent per month on the Team plans and 10 on Suite Professional, capped at 10,000 a year; more cost $1.50 each when committed or $2.00 each pay-as-you-go. Source official page fetched, 2026-11-25
  • Zendesk's help center says that since May 18, 2026 resolutions are tiered: only verified resolutions, which pass an LLM review after the conversation ends, draw down the allowance, while contained resolutions and assisted escalations do not. Source official page fetched, 2026-11-25
  • The same article says a messaging or email conversation ends 72 hours after its first message and a voice conversation ends at hang-up, which is when the resolution is judged. Source official page fetched, 2026-11-25
  • The pricing page notes that AI agents for voice require Zendesk voice, and its FAQ says the 14-day free trial opens the Suite Professional plan by default. Source official page fetched, 2026-11-25

Where it fits in an agent build

Zendesk fits a support team that already runs on tickets and wants the AI agent inside the same queue, routing and reporting. The verified-resolution rule is the most buyer-friendly definition we have seen among the per-resolution vendors in our tracker, because a conversation the reviewer cannot confirm as solved is not billed. By our arithmetic, five Suite Team agents get 25 included resolutions a month; 300 AI resolutions would add 275 at $1.50 to $2.00, or $412.50 to $550, on top of $275 in seats. (Our analysis.)

When to look elsewhere

The allowance scales with human seats, so a two-person shop gets only 10 included resolutions a month and pays for nearly every AI success. The cheapest plan, Support Team, does not list messaging, live chat or telephony (those start at Suite Team), and voice AI needs Zendesk voice as well. A team that wants a single flat bill, or that answers mostly through Instagram and website chat, may find Tidio's shared quota easier to budget. (Our analysis.)

Questions to ask before you buy

  • Which of our plans' AI agent features are available in a trial account?
  • What share of resolutions in accounts like ours end as contained rather than verified?
  • Can committed resolutions roll over if we under-use them?
  • Which Suite tier is the minimum for the HIPAA-enabled services we would need?

A setup outline

  1. Count how many human agents actually need a seat; the AI allowance and the seat bill both follow that number.
  2. Turn on the AI agent for messaging or email first, using your help center as its knowledge source.
  3. Decide between committed resolutions at $1.50 and pay-as-you-go at $2.00 before launch, based on a pilot month.
  4. Watch the split between verified and contained resolutions for a month; only verified ones are billed.
  5. Add Zendesk voice and a voice AI agent only after chat and email resolutions look reliable.
  6. Re-price at renewal with real resolution counts rather than the sales estimate.
