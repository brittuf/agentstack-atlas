Where it fits in an agent build

Upfirst's $24.95 Starter plan is the cheapest paid entry point in our receptionist tracker, which makes it a sensible test for a very small business taking a call or two a day. The $9.95 extra-agent add-on matters for multi-location operators, such as a business with two shops, who want each location answered with its own details without paying for a second full plan. (Our analysis.)

When to look elsewhere

At higher volumes the per-call overage on small plans adds up; compare the Pro and Scale tiers against per-minute options once you pass a few hundred calls a month. Check booking integrations before relying on it for appointments. (Our analysis.)

Questions to ask before you buy

  • Does a call that goes to voicemail or hangs up early count toward my 30, 90 or 300 calls?
  • Can each extra agent have its own greeting, hours and transfer number?
  • Which calendars or booking tools does it write appointments into, rather than just taking a message?
  • If I switch to annual billing for the 20% saving, can I still move between tiers mid-year?

A setup outline

  1. Count monthly calls per location.
  2. Use the 14-day trial on one location first.
  3. Set up location-specific answers (hours, address, services) and test each one.
  4. Add the second location as an extra agent only after the first works.
  5. Re-check the tier after a full month of real volume.
