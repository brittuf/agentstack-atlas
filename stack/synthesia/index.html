Where it fits in an agent build

Synthesia fits where video is a format problem, not a creative one: onboarding modules, policy updates, product how-tos, the same explainer in ten languages. The credit maths on its pricing page is concrete enough to budget: at about 100 credits per finished minute, Starter's 1,250 credits add roughly 12 minutes a month on top of the 10 free minutes. In an agent pipeline, the dubbing and avatar APIs let a workflow tool produce a localised version automatically once a script is approved. (Our analysis.)

When to look elsewhere

Brand films, ads that need real footage, or anything where a synthetic presenter would undercut trust are better made with people and cameras. If the output is mostly audio, a voice tool is cheaper. Monthly credits do not roll over, so bursty production schedules may suit annual billing, where the year's credits are available upfront. (Our analysis.)

Questions to ask before you buy

  • How many credits does a minute with the avatar on screen throughout really use?
  • Can unused monthly credits carry over in any form?
  • What consent and verification does a personal avatar require?
  • Can I cap API spend separately from studio use?

A setup outline

  1. Pick one repeatable video type, such as a two-minute onboarding module, and write three scripts.
  2. Produce them on the free Basic plan and show them to the people who will actually watch them.
  3. Choose stock or personal avatars; personal avatars come with paid plans and need the presenter's consent.
  4. Estimate monthly minutes and dubbing languages, then convert to credits at about 100 per minute and 40 to 80 per dubbed minute.
  5. Only then wire the API into a workflow tool so approved scripts become videos without manual steps.
