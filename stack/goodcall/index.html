Where it fits in an agent build

Per-caller billing changes the maths. A nine-minute booking call costs the same as a hang-up, and a regular customer who rings five times a month counts once. That suits businesses with long or repeat calls, such as property managers fielding maintenance requests from the same tenants. Goodcall's statement that spam and wrong numbers are not counted also removes a common hidden cost. (Our analysis.)

When to look elsewhere

If most of your callers are new each time and calls are short, per-minute plans can come out cheaper; run your numbers in our calculator. There is a demo agent rather than a free trial, so test it on realistic scenarios. (Our analysis.)

A setup outline

  1. Count unique callers per month from your phone records, not total calls.
  2. Try the demo agent with your three most common call types.
  3. Run the numbers in the cost calculator assuming every call is a new caller (the worst case).
  4. Ask how a caller who rings again next month is counted.
  5. Start with overflow or after-hours calls.
