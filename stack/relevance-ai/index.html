Where it fits in an agent build

The sales side of our catalog is where this kind of platform fits: Leads.io qualifying leads across thousands of campaigns, and Backstory's agent flagging account risk and next actions. A small sales team can copy the pattern by having one agent research each inbound lead, a second score it against a written ideal-customer profile, and a person decide who gets a call. Neither company's source names Relevance AI. (Our analysis.)

When to look elsewhere

Because self-serve pricing could not be verified, budget carefully before committing; ask for written pricing. Teams that only need lead scoring inside an existing CRM may find a native CRM feature or a Zapier step cheaper. (Our analysis.)

A setup outline

  1. Write your ideal-customer profile as five scoring rules a new hire could apply.
  2. Have the agent apply them to 50 past leads with known outcomes and compare.
  3. Only route leads automatically once agreement with your past outcomes is high.
  4. Let reps override scores and record why.
  5. Review false negatives weekly, since lost good leads are the expensive mistake.
