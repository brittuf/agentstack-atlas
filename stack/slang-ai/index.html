Where it fits in an agent build

Full-service restaurants get the same handful of calls all night: is there a table at eight, are you open on the holiday, can I bring a cake, where do I park. Slang AI's value is answering those from approved information and writing bookings into the reservation system the restaurant already uses. For groups, cross-location suggestions are the distinctive feature: when the first choice is full, the agent offers a sister restaurant instead of losing the cover. (Our analysis.)

When to look elsewhere

Takeout-heavy spots need an ordering agent that writes into the POS and takes payment. Slang's materials focus on reservations, and a competitor's roundup says it does not take phone orders (a vendor-written comparison, so ask Slang directly). At $399 per location and up, a small cafe with few calls may not recover the cost. (Our analysis.)

Questions to ask before you buy

  • Is the per-location price monthly, and what is the contract length?
  • Which features need Premium rather than Core?
  • How are takeout calls handled: transfer, text link or message?
  • Can we edit the approved answers ourselves?

A setup outline

  1. Count last month's calls by type: reservations, hours and directions, takeout, private events.
  2. Confirm your reservation platform is one Slang integrates with.
  3. Write approved answers for the top 20 questions, including holiday hours and policies.
  4. Route VIP and private-event calls to a manager; let the agent handle routine bookings.
  5. Compare covers booked by phone and unanswered calls for the month before and after.
