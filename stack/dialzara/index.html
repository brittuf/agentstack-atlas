Where it fits in an agent build

Per-minute billing favours businesses with many short calls: quick availability checks, hours, directions, simple booking requests. The detail to check is transfer type. On the entry plan a transfer is blind, meaning the caller is passed on with no context, while Pro adds warm transfer, where the person picking up hears who is calling and why. For a trade business where a missed emergency call costs a job, that difference matters more than the per-minute rate. (Our analysis.)

When to look elsewhere

If your calls are long (detailed quotes, troubleshooting) per-minute billing climbs fast; per-caller pricing like Goodcall's may be cheaper. If you need booking straight into ServiceTitan or a dental practice system, check native options first. (Our analysis.)

A setup outline

  1. Pull last month's call log and work out total talk minutes, not just call count.
  2. Run the 7-day trial on after-hours calls only.
  3. Test both a routine call and an urgent one, and listen to how each transfer sounds.
  4. Connect Zapier or Make so every call summary lands in your CRM or a sheet.
  5. Compare the month's minutes with the plan tiers before choosing Lite or Pro.
