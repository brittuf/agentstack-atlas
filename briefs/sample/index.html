Snapshot brief: HVAC company, Melbourne, FL

This is an illustrative sample. The client, "Example Air Co.", is fictional: a made-up independent HVAC company with 4 technicians in Melbourne, FL. It doesn't describe any real business. 1. Summary

  1. Published prices get you named by AI search. When we asked an AI search engine what maintenance plans cost in Melbourne, every company it quoted prints a plan price on its own page (for example $149, $199–$547 and $250 a year). Companies without a published price weren't mentioned.
  2. Posted hours decide who gets the emergency calls. For a 24/7 emergency query, the AI answer named companies with explicit 24/7 pages and called out one highly rated competitor as "not a reliable 24/7 option" because it closes on Sundays.
  3. Fast reply is a real gap. One well-reviewed competitor's form promises a reply "within 24 hours". Public reviews in this market keep praising same-day callbacks and same-day fixes.

#1 move this month: cover after-hours calls with an AI receptionist that can book or escalate emergencies (about $49–$149/mo), and publish one page with your 24/7 policy and maintenance-plan price.

5. Customer voice

Themes from recent public reviews of Brevard County HVAC companies shown in a public places listing listing source via search extract:

  • Same-day fixes win praise: "fixed the issue the same day"; "came out for a quote the day we called".
  • Honesty and fair pricing: "didn't rip me off like the other companies have done"; "very reasonably priced".
  • People remember the technician or owner by name: many reviews name the tech who came out ("Tom", "Jim"). Personal follow-up gets mentioned too.
  • Fast callbacks, even on weekends: one reviewer left a website message on the weekend and got a call back on Sunday to book.

These quotes are about other local companies and show what customers here value. A paid brief mines your own reviews and your competitors' reviews.
