AI citation readiness checker

Paste a page's text or HTML and get a score plus a checklist. The checks come from the research summarized in our GEO playbook. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Heuristic, not a guarantee. No tool can promise that ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google AI Overviews will cite a page. This checker only looks for features that published studies associate with being used in AI answers. Ranking in normal search, crawler access and mentions on other sites matter too, and a pasted snippet can't show those.


What is scored, and why

CheckPointsEvidence
Concrete statistics (percentages, money, counts)25Supported in the KDD 2024 GEO paper and still helped on every platform in a 3,205-page replication (replication).
Substantial length (600+ words)15High-influence pages were longer (arXiv 2604.25707).
Modular structure (3+ section headings)15High-influence pages were modular (same study).
Definitions10Rich in definitions (same study).
Comparisons10Rich in comparisons (same study).
Procedural steps10Rich in procedural steps (same study).
Structured data (Article, Organization or FAQ JSON-LD)15A technical basic from our playbook. This is practitioner guidance, not a measured effect.

Flagged but not scored: heavy quotation or citation stuffing, which didn't help in the replication and often went the wrong direction; Q&A formatting alone, which the 2026 study found didn't help; and a visible "updated" date. Freshness isn't in our evidence base, so it's shown for information only.
