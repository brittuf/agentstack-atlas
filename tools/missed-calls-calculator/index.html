What are missed calls costing you?

Estimate monthly and yearly revenue lost to unanswered calls, then compare that to AI receptionist plan prices from our tracker. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. Results are shareable via the URL.






How the math works (your inputs, not a forecast): missed calls/week = calls × miss% ÷ 100. Estimated jobs lost/week = missed × close% ÷ 100. Monthly lost revenue ≈ jobs/week × (52÷12) × job value. Yearly ≈ jobs/week × 52 × job value. The AI cost column uses the same plan math as our AI receptionist pricing tracker: cheapest listed plan at your recovered-call volume (missed calls treated as calls/month the agent would answer), with the call-length field converting to talk minutes for per-minute vendors.

Sourced benchmarks you can paste in (or ignore):

  • Missed / unanswered: Invoca's May 23, 2024 home-services post reported 27% of calls unanswered on its platform (Invoca). Invoca's 2026 Home Services Lead Conversion Benchmarks Report says 52% of callers speak with a person across its home-services customer base (so 48% do not), rising to 65%/73% answer rates when filtering calls over 15/30 seconds (Invoca 2026 report). CallRail's Jan 14, 2025 release reported a 14% missed-call rate for home services in its sample. These are vendor platform averages, not your shop.
  • After-hours share: ServiceTitan reported 10.7%–12.1% of trades calls outside business hours in a sample of its customer accounts (May 2025–July 2026 fiscal window on their page) (ServiceTitan). Useful if you only want to price after-hours coverage.
  • Booking rate context: In the same ServiceTitan piece, shops with 10 or fewer technicians booked 30% of calls (median-based; their disclaimer says not industry-representative). The default close rate here is a user-adjustable example, not a cited industry average.
  • Job value and weekly call volume: defaults are user-adjustable examples (same spirit as the example volumes on our trade cost pages). Replace them with your own numbers from your phone system or CRM.
  • Call length: default 3 minutes matches the example on our receptionist calculator; it is an assumption for plan math, not a measured average.
