Morningstar: how the agent works and how to build one like it

Summary: Morningstar built an advisor assistant that handles portfolio review, risk, research and compliance prep with audit trails, according to the source.

Source: https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/industries/how-morningstar-built-a-financial-advisor-ai-assistant-powered-by-amazon-bedrock-agentcore/ source read via search extract

Why this use case works

Audit trails are what make it deployable in a regulated advisory setting. Every generated output can be traced back to its inputs and sources for compliance review. (Our analysis.)

Agent pattern: Document-review agent

A document-processing agent: ingestion and OCR, extraction into a schema, rule or model-based review, and a human approval step before anything binding goes out. Audit logs are a requirement, not a nice-to-have.

How a smaller team could replicate it

Wealth and advisory practices can begin with meeting-prep summaries built from portfolio data and research, logging every source used, and keep any client-facing recommendation behind advisor approval. (Our analysis, not a claim made by the source.)

Risks and guardrails

Risks: unsuitable recommendations, hallucinated figures in client material, and record-keeping rules. Guardrails: every number traced to a data source, advisor approval on client-facing output, and retention of prompts and outputs under the firm's compliance policy. (Our analysis.)

What to measure

Prep time per client meeting, compliance review exceptions, advisor adoption, and accuracy spot checks.
