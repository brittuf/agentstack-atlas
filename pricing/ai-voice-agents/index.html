If you're building your own phone agent instead of buying a packaged AI receptionist, the platforms charge by the minute, and the headline rate is rarely the whole bill. Every number here comes from the vendor's own pricing page today.

Which one fits?

  • Testing a first agent: Retell ($0 to start), Bland Start (no card) and Vapi ($5 credits) all let you build before paying. Synthflow doesn't.
  • You want one predictable rate: Bland quotes one per-minute number that includes the model and voice. Retell and Vapi let you swap models and voices, which can make them cheaper or pricier.
  • High volume: Bland Build's $299 fee pays for itself past about 15,000 minutes a month (2 cents saved per minute). Vapi Pro's $999 minimum is for teams that need its SLA and retention, not for saving money.
  • A small business that just wants calls answered: you probably don't need a developer platform. See AI receptionist pricing for flat monthly plans, or run your numbers in the missed-calls calculator.

Our view (opinion): the LLM choice moves the bill more than the platform does. On Retell, picking GPT 5 mini ($0.008/min) instead of Claude 5.5 Sonnet ($0.064/min) moves the LLM line by $0.056 a minute, and prompts over 4,000 tokens are billed as extra time. Price a real script, not the headline.
