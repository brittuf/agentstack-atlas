Five "describe it and get an app" builders, every price taken from the vendor's own pricing page or docs today. Some charge per person, some charge the whole workspace, and that changes the bill more than the sticker price.

Team cost calculator

The big difference between these tools is who pays: Lovable (and, as far as their pages say, Base44 and Replit Pro) price the workspace, while Bolt and v0 charge per member. Enter your team size to see the entry paid plan's monthly bill for each.

List prices for the cheapest paid plan only. Excludes taxes, usage beyond included credits or tokens, and Enterprise. Usage units differ (credits, tokens, message credits), so a cheaper plan can run out faster; check each vendor's notes below.

Which one fits?

  • A small business with 2-5 people poking at one internal tool: per-workspace pricing (Lovable Pro, $25/mo for 100 shared credits) is the cheapest way in, but the whole team draws down one balance. Set per-member credit limits.
  • One founder shipping a prototype: every tool here has a free tier except Replit (none shown). Start free and upgrade when you hit the daily cap.
  • Designers and front-end teams already on Vercel: v0 deploys straight to Vercel, at $30 per user.
  • Teams where each person builds separately: Bolt Teams ($30/member) gives each paid member their own token allowance, which avoids one person draining a shared pool.
  • Non-technical owner who wants backend, auth and database without setup: Base44 and Lovable both bundle them; Base44's bigger plans are quoted annually.

Our view (opinion): the cost that surprises people is usage, not the plan. Each builder burns credits or tokens per prompt, so build one real feature on the free tier, check what it cost, then size the plan. For developer teams, see AI coding agent pricing.
