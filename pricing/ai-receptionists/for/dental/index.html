What to check before you buy

  • Patient data: ask the vendor in writing whether it will sign a HIPAA business associate agreement before any patient details flow through it.
  • PMS sync: check whether it books into your practice management system or just collects a callback request.
  • Recall and reminders: some tools also run outbound reminder calls; price those separately.
