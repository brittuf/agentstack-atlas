How to pick

  • Short calls, many callers: per-minute plans (Dialzara, Rosie) usually win.
  • Long calls, repeat customers: per-caller billing (Goodcall) can be cheaper, because a nine-minute booking costs the same as a hang-up.
  • Low volume: Smith.ai's free 25 calls or Upfirst's $24.95 Starter are the cheapest way to test.
  • Check booking: some entry plans only take messages or text a link instead of booking into your calendar.
  • Trade software: if you run ServiceTitan, Tekmetric or a dental PMS, see our trade guides for native options.

Our view (opinion): start with after-hours and overflow calls only, measure missed-call recovery for a month, then decide whether to move daytime calls over. Use the ROI calculator to check payback.
