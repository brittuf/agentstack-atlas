Which plan fits?

  • Solo builder testing agents: start on a free tier (Copilot, Codex, Devin, Jules and Kiro all have one), then pay $8-$20 when you hit limits. Claude Code needs at least Pro.
  • Small team (2-10 devs) on GitHub: Copilot Business at $19/seat is the cheapest per-seat team plan we found, with admin controls and a pooled credit budget.
  • Team that wants one agent in chat and code: ChatGPT Business (Codex) and Claude Team Standard are $20/seat on annual billing, $25 monthly.
  • A few heavy agent users: give them a bigger seat (Claude Team Premium, Cursor Super/Ultra, Copilot Max, Kiro Power) and keep everyone else on the entry seat. Use the mix-and-match rows above to price that.
  • Non-developers building internal apps: Replit's Core/Pro bundle hosting and databases, so there's less to set up. Read the agent's changes before anything touches customer data.
  • Bigger teams (~20+ seats): Devin's $80 base fee gets spread thin as seats go up, but compare usage rules, not just list price.
  • Needs SSO, audit logs, data residency: that is usually Enterprise, which every vendor here quotes privately.

Our view (opinion): list price is the smaller part of the bill. Every tool here meters agent work (credits, pools, task caps), so run a two-week pilot on real tickets, watch the usage dashboard, then pick seats. For non-coding automation, compare against Zapier, Make and n8n.
