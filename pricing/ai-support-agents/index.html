AI support agent pricing, per resolution, decoded.

Every AI support agent says you only pay when it works. Then each one counts "works" differently: an outcome, an automated resolution, a session, an interaction, a conversation. Same 1,000 chats, five different bills. Every number here comes from the vendor's own pricing page today.

Checked 2026-11-23. Where a vendor publishes no price, we say "quote only" instead of guessing. Confirm before you buy. We haven't benchmarked resolution quality. This page has no affiliate links today; if that changes, links will be labeled per our disclosure.

Monthly cost calculator

Each vendor bills a different unit: Intercom per outcome, Zendesk per automated resolution, Freshdesk per AI session, Gorgias per automated interaction plus tickets, Tidio per conversation. Enter your volume once and we apply each vendor's own unit at list price.

Annual-billing seat prices where the vendor shows them (Intercom, Zendesk, Freshdesk, Gorgias helpdesk; Tidio annual = 10 x monthly / 12). Gorgias Starter is monthly-only. Excludes taxes, add-ons, voice, Copilot seats, free trial allowances and negotiated deals. Ada, Decagon and Sierra publish no rates, so they aren't in the calculator.

Which one fits?

  • Small store or small business, under 1,000 chats: Tidio's conversation quota is the simplest bill to predict, because AI and human chats come out of one number. Gorgias is built for Shopify-style ecommerce and charges by tickets, not seats.
  • Your AI resolves a lot: per-resolution pricing (Intercom, Zendesk, Gorgias) grows with success. At a high resolution rate, Freshdesk's per-session price ($0.49) can be cheaper because it doesn't change with the outcome.
  • Your AI resolves little at first: per-session and per-conversation billing charges you for the attempts too. Per-resolution billing charges you only when it works, by the vendor's definition, so read that definition.
  • You already have a helpdesk: Intercom sells Fin on top of Salesforce and others at $0.99 per outcome with no seats, subject to a monthly minimum.
  • Enterprise, voice plus chat plus email: Ada, Decagon and Sierra all sell by contract. Ask each for its rate and its written definition of a resolution.

Our view (opinion): the unit matters more than the price. Intercom counts an outcome when a customer "doesn't ask for more help after Fin responds", and Decagon's own pricing post points out that a customer who gives up and leaves can be counted as resolved. Before you compare $0.99 to $2.00, ask each vendor how many of last month's "resolutions" reopened.
