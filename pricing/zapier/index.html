Zapier pricing

No-code automation (9,000+ apps) with Zapier Agents and MCP.

Checked 2026-10-18 from the official page: https://zapier.com/pricing. Prices change; confirm on the vendor's site before buying. Where we couldn't verify a figure, we say "see vendor" instead of guessing.

Plans

PlanPriceWhat's included
Free$0100 tasks/mo, two-step Zaps, basic access to Agents, Chatbots, MCP
Professional$19.99/mo billed annually ($29.99 monthly)750 tasks/mo entry tier; multi-step Zaps, premium apps, webhooks
Team$69/mo billed annually ($103.50 monthly)2,000 tasks/mo entry tier; 25 users, shared workspace, SAML SSO
EnterpriseCustom (contact sales)Unlimited users, annual task limits, SCIM

Billing unit

Per task (each successful action step); annual billing is 33% off monthly.

Notes

  • Overage (pay-per-task) is 1.25x base rate on annual, 2.5x on monthly plans.
  • Zapier Agents bill in activities, separate from tasks: Free 400/mo, Pro 1,500/mo.
  • 14-day Professional trial, no card required.
